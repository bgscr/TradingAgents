# Require evidence-bound directional justification

TradingAgents will permit a Trading Decision only when its direction is justified by explicit Decision Assertions over validated Material Evidence. Unvalidated analyst reports, debates, and prior-decision prose may generate hypotheses, but they cannot influence final direction unless promoted into validated evidence; citation-only gating is rejected because it proves provenance without proving that the evidence supports the rating. If a directional justification cannot be expressed and validated, the run produces a non-directional Analysis Outcome.

## Consequences

The system may abstain more often, and a rating cannot become immutable before its Decision Assertions pass validation. Evaluations must test invariance to unsupported prose and reject arbitrary rating swaps over an unchanged evidence ledger.
