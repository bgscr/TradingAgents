# Gate synthesis on material evidence

TradingAgents will use source-local validation plus two deterministic evidence gates: an admission gate before debate and a decision gate against the material premises of a draft thesis. Missing optional evidence may degrade confidence, but insufficient or conflicted Material Evidence prevents a Trading Decision and produces a non-directional Analysis Outcome instead; this avoids both indiscriminate blocking and recommendations founded on unsupported facts.

## Consequences

Analysts must expose source-linked material claims in structured form. A draft may be revised once to remove unsupported premises, but `Hold` cannot stand in for insufficient evidence because it is itself a portfolio recommendation.
